Analytics on this site

We use our own small analytics script (not Google Analytics or ad trackers). It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. It stores two kinds of item in your browser:

  • A random session ID in sessionStorage, deleted when you close the tab.
  • Campaign source tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, or ref) from the link you arrived by, also in sessionStorage and cleared when you close the tab, so that we can count which campaigns and pages lead to visits and sales.

For each page view or marked click we record: the event type and which link was clicked, the page path, the referring page, the campaign tags, the session ID, a rough country (if our host supplies it), whether the device looks like a mobile, and a coarse browser family (for example Chrome, Firefox or a search-engine bot, never the full browser string or version). On our free tools we also record which options you pick, for example the model, its provider and whether you entered a cap on the AI API cost calculator, but not the numbers you type. Events go to our own endpoint and database hosted by Neon (Neon Postgres, EU – Frankfurt region), which processes the data for us.

Why: only to produce aggregate statistics about how the site is used (which pages and campaigns work) so we can improve the site and our products. We do not use this to identify you, build profiles, advertise, or sell data. We rely on the "statistical purposes" exception in PECR (as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025), so we don't ask for consent, but you can object.

Lawful basis (UK GDPR): legitimate interests (understanding and improving our site). The data is pseudonymous: no name, email or IP address.

How to object (free, one click): use the Turn off analytics on this browser link in the footer of every page and on this page. It stops the script storing or sending anything and clears the session ID and tags. To remember your choice we store one small "analytics off" flag in your browser; clearing your browser storage removes it. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat that as an objection automatically and the script does not run.

Retention: raw analytics events are deleted after 13 months; only aggregated counts are kept after that.
