FAQ

Do I need a paid Notion plan?
No, both versions work on Notion's free plan.
Does it sync with Stripe or Gumroad?
No. You log sales manually or with your own automation (Zapier or Make).
Does it handle multiple currencies?
Each entry has a currency tag, but totals add up the numbers you enter, so use one home currency for accurate totals.
What is the difference between Lite and Pro?
Lite covers projects, tasks, subscriptions and a weekly review. Pro adds revenue with MRR and VAT, expenses, profit per product, invoices, bugs, releases and a dashboard.
Is it accounting software?
No. It's an organisation and tracking tool, not tax or accounting advice.
