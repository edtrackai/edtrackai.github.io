The Indie Developer's Subscription Audit: Find and Cut Wasted Tool Spend

Hosting, domains, AI assistants, analytics, email, monitoring, design tools: individually they're small, but together they're often the biggest cost of a solo software business. Yearly renewals arrive exactly when you've forgotten about them. Here's a 20-minute audit, then a system that keeps it under control.

This guide pairs with Solo Founder OS, and if you want the next step, read Notion Template for Indie Hackers: What to Look For (and What to Skip).

Step 1: List everything (10 minutes)

Go through the last 12 months of:

  • Card and bank statements (search "monthly", "annual", "subscription")
  • PayPal → Settings → Automatic payments
  • App Store / Google Play subscriptions
  • Your email for "receipt", "renewal" and "invoice"

For each one record: name, cost, billing period, next renewal date and which project it serves.

Step 2: Normalise to monthly

Yearly plans hide their real cost. Divide yearly by 12. Mark trials separately and set cancelled plans to £0 so they stop counting but stay on record.

Cost per month = if(prop("Status") == "Cancelled", 0,
  if(prop("Billing") == "Yearly", prop("Cost") / 12, prop("Cost")))

Step 3: Add a renewal countdown

Days to renewal = dateBetween(prop("Next renewal"), today(), "days")

Renewing soon = and(prop("Status") != "Cancelled",
  dateBetween(prop("Next renewal"), today(), "days") >= 0,
  dateBetween(prop("Next renewal"), today(), "days") <= 30)

A calendar view on Next renewal makes the year visible at a glance.

Step 4: Assign each tool to a project

Link every subscription to the project it serves and roll up Cost per month onto your projects. This is the step that changes decisions: it's easy to justify £15/month "for the business", much harder to justify it for a side project with no users.

Step 5: Decide, for each tool

QuestionIf no…
Did I use it in the last 30 days?Cancel, or downgrade to free
Is there a free tier that covers my usage?Downgrade
Do two tools overlap?Keep one
Is the yearly plan cheaper and will I definitely keep it?Stay monthly

Step 6: A 15-minute weekly review

Every Friday: check "renewing soon", cancel what you don't need before it renews, and update next-renewal dates after each charge.

UK note

If you're a UK sole trader, subscriptions used for business are usually allowable expenses. Keep receipts and apportion mixed personal/business use. This isn't tax advice, so check HMRC guidance for your situation.
